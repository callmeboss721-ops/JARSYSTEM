---
name: YOUNGBOSS brand intent
description: Product-specific visual direction and identity constraints for Jaguar Kratom House
---

JAGUAR KRATOM HOUSE should feel like a premium YOUNGBOSS membership in a
luxury dark / midnight executive style: restrained, professional, modern, and
easy to read in Thai. Do not turn its loyalty stamps into a game or generic
cyberpunk interface. Avoid cartoon animal graphics; jaguar identity should be
an extremely subtle motif, spot texture, or silhouette.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this direction instead of the original
neon-green/cartoon-jaguar presentation.

**How to apply:** Keep customer and staff experiences visually related while
clearly separating their purposes. Use the app's shared tokens for the exact
palette; gold is the main brand accent, emerald secondary, and cyan is limited
to necessary status/interaction feedback. Do not treat a polished UI as proof
that the underlying Supabase connection or real transactions work.
