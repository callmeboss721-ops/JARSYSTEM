---
name: YOUNGBOSS brand intent
description: Product-specific visual direction and identity constraints for Jaguar Kratom House
---

JAGUAR KRATOM HOUSE should feel like a premium YOUNGBOSS membership in a
luxury dark / midnight executive style: restrained, professional, modern, and
easy to read in Thai. Do not turn its loyalty stamps into a game or generic
cyberpunk interface. Keep decorative jaguar motifs subtle, but use the user's
original supplied logo imagery rather than inventing a replacement emblem.

**Why:** The user requested a restrained premium interface, then explicitly
clarified that the logo must retain the original image theme. The logo
requirement takes precedence over avoiding illustrative artwork in that asset.

**How to apply:** Keep customer and staff experiences visually related while
clearly separating their purposes. Use the app's shared tokens for the exact
palette; gold is the main brand accent, emerald secondary, and cyan is limited
to necessary status/interaction feedback. Do not treat a polished UI as proof
that the underlying Supabase connection or real transactions work.

The user permits an alternative to the blocked Supabase implementation and
prioritizes a usable system, not continued attempts to force that integration.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this after the connection blockers were
explained.

**How to apply:** Confirm storage choice and treatment of existing member data
before replacing the database. Do not silently migrate or discard old balances.
