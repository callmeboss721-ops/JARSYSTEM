---
name: Quick-sale stock policy
description: User-selected stock handling for JAGUAR product sales that do not earn loyalty points.
---

For non-loyalty product sales, record the sale even when tracked stock is insufficient, never let stock go below zero, and warn staff to recount.

**Why:** the user selected this policy for fast sales that do not involve point collection.

**How to apply:** keep this policy limited to non-loyalty product sales; do not change member-point sales behavior.
