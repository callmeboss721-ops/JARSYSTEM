---
name: Artwork claim approvals
description: How to apply owner approval for text embedded in customer-facing images.
---

Use only the exact customer-facing image text that the owner approved in the JAGUAR SPK knowledge base. An approval does not establish related product details, delivery areas or conditions, membership eligibility, or benefits unless those are separately recorded.

**Why:** Uploaded artwork can contain product, service, or membership claims that are not otherwise supported by the image itself.

**How to apply:** Check the approval status in `docs/agent-guides/jaguar-spk-knowledge-base.md` before displaying or reusing any embedded wording; keep operational terms distinct from approval of a short label.
